Comp Collector

One-click tool that reads sold listings from eBay or 130point and sends them directly to CardGrade Pro — no copy/paste needed.

Step 1 — Install once
📎 Comp Collector
↑ drag to your bookmarks bar
Show bar: Chrome Ctrl+Shift+B · Mac ⌘ Shift B
2
Open eBay Sold Listings
In CardGrade Pro, enter your card details and click Open eBay Sold ↗. eBay will open in a new tab showing sold comps for that card across all grades.
3
Click "Comp Collector" in Your Bookmarks Bar
While on the eBay (or 130point) sold results page, click the Comp Collector bookmark. It scans each listing title to detect the grade (PSA 10, PSA 9, etc.), skips lots, reprints, and non-PSA listings, then shows you a confirmation with a per-grade breakdown before sending.
4
Switch Back and Click Calculate
Return to CardGrade Pro — comps are auto-filled into every grade bucket. Review the comp preview, remove any outliers with ✕, then click ⚡ Calculate for ROI, break-even, and a buy/pass recommendation.
